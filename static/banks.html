Accepted columns / keys

title (required), description, category (“Parent > Child” makes a hierarchy), projects (separate with ;), tags (; or , separated), author, primaryContact, secondaryContact, status (new / exploring / in-progress / done / shelved), related (titles of other ideas, ; separated), createdAt.
JSON may be a plain array of ideas or {"ideas":[…],"projects":[…],"categories":[…]}.
